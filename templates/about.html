About Silver Predictor

Silver Predictor is an AI-based forecasting dashboard that estimates direction (UP/DOWN) for silver price over different horizons, and shows estimated prices using state/UT premium and purity.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Predictions are based on historical patterns and available market data. They are for educational/reference use only.
