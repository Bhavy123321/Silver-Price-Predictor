About Silver Predictor

This project predicts the direction of silver price movement (UP/DOWN) using historical trends + live market signals. It also shows estimated prices for 1g/10g/100g with state premium and purity.

What the model does

  • Predicts UP/DOWN direction
  • Gives confidence score
  • Shows trend chart

Important note

All prices are estimates and may differ from local retail markets. Use as a reference only.
